Confocal ND2 Visualizer V3

Library

Top level is the type of confocal imaging (Immunostaining, Live-cell, Whole-mount — add your own). Inside each, link experiment folders. Inside an experiment, create channel sub-folders (named from the gene/antibody of each channel) and drop the matching ND2 files in. Tag any sub-folder with a reusable project. Nothing is uploaded.
